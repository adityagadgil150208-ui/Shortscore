A LITTLE KNOW-HOW

You don’t need to speak Wall Street.

Here’s what helps when you’re reading a short seller report.

01

Understand who’s talking

A short seller can profit when a stock falls. A published report explains why they think that might happen. That financial interest is useful context.

02

Separate a claim from a fact

A report presents the author’s view. Look for original documents, read the company’s response, and check dates. An AI summary does not verify the claims.

03

Use a summary as your starting point

Explore our labelled examples or paste a report to summarize it. We explain its claims and suggest questions to research. We don’t predict prices or tell you what to buy or sell.

Our example library is illustrative historical content, not a live feed. Reports you summarize stay in server memory until it restarts. Saved bookmarks stay in this browser.
